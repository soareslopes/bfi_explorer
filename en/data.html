Reproducibility

How these files were produced

The full pipeline, from GIS processing to the final tally, is in Python. The files published here are produced by build_dados_site.py from the pipeline output, with no manual step.

Bike Friendly Index

An index of how friendly each mainland Portuguese municipality is to cycling as a mode of urban transport. 2018 and 2025 editions.
