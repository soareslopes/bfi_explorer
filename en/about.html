The project

Who makes the BFI

The Bike Friendly Index is an initiative of the BEAM research group at the Faculty of Architecture, University of Lisbon, drawing on years of research and practice in active mobility.

Team

A. P. Figueiredo, D. S. Vale and A. S. Lopes.
BEAM · Faculty of Architecture · University of Lisbon

Editions

The first edition was published in 2018, with 12 indicators and 2011 Census data. The 2025 edition widens the set to 25 indicators, refreshes every underlying source and rebalances the weights of the five dimensions.

The aim is to recompute the index regularly, so as to track how the national picture evolves and to identify the municipalities that stand out in promoting cycling.

Corrections

If you spot an error or an omission, in the data or on the site, we want to hear about it. Because the index rests on open data, a correction can be folded back into the source database and show up in the next edition.
