What the 2018 report said

A country at the start of the road

In 2018 there were about 360 km of cycle lanes inside mainland urban areas, municipal spending on sustainable mobility over 2015-2017 came to roughly 50 million euros, and women made up about 15% of cyclists.

In 2025 urban cycle lanes reach 1,317 km, spending has risen to 69.9 million euros, and 99 municipalities run a bike sharing scheme.
